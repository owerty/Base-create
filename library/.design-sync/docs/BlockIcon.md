A small square swatch of a block's side texture (default 16px, pixelated), the same swatch the material list uses. Standalone. Unknown references render as a magenta square. Useful for legends, palettes and material callouts next to a `<Structure>`.

```tsx
<BlockIcon block="minecraft:stonebrick@0" />
<BlockIcon block="chisel:marble@0" size={32} />
```
